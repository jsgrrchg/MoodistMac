# Moodist for iPhone — privacy policy

Moodist plays audio bundled with the application. It does not require an account, include advertising, track you or send listening history, mixes or preferences to the developer.

Your selections, favorites, custom mixes, settings and timer state are stored in the application’s local storage. They may be included in device backups managed by Apple according to your device settings. Moodist does not provide its own cloud synchronization.

Importing a preference file reads the file you select. Exporting or sharing writes the mixes and favorites you choose to transfer. The destination app or file provider processes that file under its own policy. Links to source code and support open an external website.

Notification permission is optional and used for timer completion alerts. Microphone, photo-library and music-library access are not requested for playback.

You can remove mixes/favorites or restore defaults in Moodist’s settings. Deleting the app removes its local application data, subject to backups controlled by your device and cloud provider.

Support: https://github.com/jsgrrchg/MoodistMac/issues
